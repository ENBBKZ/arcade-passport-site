03 / Built for both sides

Good for the game. Good for the room.

For gamers

Keep the nights that count.

  • A play history across participating arcade bars.
  • Venue map, leaderboards, and monthly recap in the planned app.
  • Share your public profile by tapping your keychain to another person's phone.
  • Choose which Steam, PlayStation, Xbox, and Battle.net usernames are public.
For venues

Know your floor better.

  • A small reader designed to mount on each cabinet.
  • A tablet kiosk for players to set up profiles on-site.
  • A dashboard for plays per machine, peak hours, and top players.
  • Planned to be free for venues at launch; a venue subscription may follow later.

07 / FAQ

The practical stuff.

Do two keychains tap each other?

No. Both keychains are passive. To share a profile, one player taps their keychain to another person's phone. Anyone can view the public profile; adding a player requires an account.

Will a phone open a profile automatically?

On newer iPhones, a keychain tap shows a notification that the person opens to view the profile. Older phones may need the app open. Phone behavior varies by model and settings.

Does every play include a score or play duration?

No. Each cabinet tap logs a play with its game, venue, machine, and time. Score tracking is planned only for machines that can support it. A tap does not measure play duration.

Is Arcade Passport available now?

Not yet. Arcade Passport is in development. There are no live venue claims or launch dates here. Join the waitlist when it opens for updates.

What if I lose my keychain?

You can revoke a lost keychain and replace it without losing your history.

Will venues pay to participate?

The plan is to make it free for venues at launch. A venue subscription may be added later; details are subject to change.
